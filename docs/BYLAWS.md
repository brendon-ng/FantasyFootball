### 1.2 Scoring Format

The scoring format shall be **Head to Head, Points, 1 Point per Reception**.

Teams face off against each other each week of the NFL season, with the team scoring the most fantasy points winning the matchup. The team's record consists of the matchups they have won, lost, or tied.

### 1.3 League Manager

The league shall have a **League Manager** responsible for overseeing the smooth operation of the league, with duties and permissions including but not limited to:

1. **Roster Management:** May adjust team rosters, but only to resolve technical issues or under special circumstances.
2. **Trades:** Has the authority to veto trades if necessary.
3. **Scoring Adjustments:** Can adjust scoring settings or correct errors during the season.
4. **Scheduling:** Sets the semi-randomized schedule, determines the method for draft order, and decides the playoff schedule with input from the league.
5. **Rule Enforcement:** Enforces penalties for rule violations.
6. **League Communication:** Responsible for communicating important updates and decisions to the league.
7. **Dispute Resolution:** Has the final say in disputes unless otherwise specified by the league rules.
8. **League Settings:** Any changes to league settings during the season require approval by an official vote of more than **50%**.
9. **Appointment of Co-Managers:** May appoint co-managers or assistants to help with their duties.

#### 1.3.1 Removal and Replacement of the League Manager

The League Manager may be removed from their position with due cause by an official vote of more than **65%** of the teams of the league. If the League Manager is removed, the league will hold a vote to appoint a new League Manager. The previous League Manager must ensure a smooth transition of responsibilities to the new manager.

### 1.4 Rosters

Rosters will consist of 17 active players plus 2 Injured Reserve spots.

1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 2 FLEX (RB/WR/TE), 1 D/ST, 1 K, 7 Bench

#### 1.4.1 Injured Reserve

Players are eligible to be placed on Injured Reserve if they are on NFL's injured reserve or PUP lists or marked as "Out". Suspended players are not eligible.

If an ineligible player is in an Injured Reserve spot, no moves can be made by that team until the player is removed.

### 1.6 Transactions

#### 1.6.1 Lineups

Individual players are locked in their positions at the start of their respective game. Once locked they cannot be moved or dropped.

#### 1.6.2 Acquisitions and Waivers

**Player Acquisition System:** Free Agent Acquisition Budget

1. Each team is given a budget to bid on unclaimed players that are on waivers
2. Season Waiver Budget: $100
3. Minimum Bid: $0
4. Bid Tiebreaker: If two teams tie for the winning bid, the team higher on the waiver priority will receive the player.
5. Budget does NOT roll over season to season
6. No Limit on Season Acquisitions
7. Unowned players are put on waivers when their game starts
8. Waivers process and are cleared **Wednesdays at 12AM PST**
9. If a player has been owned for more than 24 hours and is dropped, he is placed on waivers for 2 days

#### 1.6.3 Trades

1. No Trade Limit
2. Trade Deadline: Week 11 - Wednesday @ 12:00 PM PST
3. Trade Review Period: 24 hours
   1. **Trade Processing:** By Sleeper default, trades will process at **midnight** following the end of the Trade Review Period.
   2. **Early Trade Processing:** By request to the Commissioner and consent from all teams involved in the trade, it can be processed immediately by the Commissioner given that the trade has passed the Trade Review Period or has substantial "Pass" votes from league members such that the "Veto" vote threshold cannot be reached.
4. Vetoes: League members can vote to veto a trade - **5 votes to veto**

### 1.7 Keepers

Each offseason, teams will be allowed to retain a set number of players from their roster each season, maintaining continuity year over year. Teams can keep up to a set number of players, with each player costing the team a draft pick in the offseason draft. The keeper deadline will be 3 days before the annual draft. After the keeper deadline, a list of all the keepers will be sent to the league. **The draft order is determined after the keeper deadline (See Appendix A).**

#### 1.7.1 Keeper Maximum

Teams can keep up to 4 players each offseason.

#### 1.7.2 Cost

Each player kept will cost the team to lose one draft pick in that offseason's draft.

1. Players have a designated draft round value, and keeping a player will cost the team its draft pick in that round.
   1. If the team does not own a pick in the designated round, or if the team keeps more players with the same round value than it has picks in that round, the cost will be the team's next available pick in an earlier round.
   2. If an earlier pick is not available, the keeper selection may not be made.
2. A player will be valued at the round they are drafted in.
   1. Keepers can be retained for up to 2 times at their original draft round cost, after which their draft round value is reset to the current offseason's ADP ("Keeper contract").
      1. The ADP will be determined by Sleeper ADP one week before the keeper deadline. Round by round ADP will be sent annually to the league one week before the keeper deadline starting in year 4.
   2. If you have multiple picks in the same round as the round of your keeper and are only planning on using one of those picks for your keeper, the keeper is assigned the lower draft slot.
      1. For example, if you have 3.05 and 3.10 and are using a third round keeper on Josh Allen, Josh Allen will be drafted with the 3.10 pick.
   3. Once the keeper deadline has passed, all keepers will manually be assigned a draft slot. **These picks can no longer be traded as they will be frozen and used to select your keeper.** See Appendix A.
3. Every time a player is drafted (not kept) in an offseason or is picked up, his value and Keeper Contract are reset.
4. Waiver/Free Agent pickups will be evaluated as follows:
   1. Undrafted Free Agents will be valued at an 11th Round value (first bench spot outside of the starting roster).
   2. Drafted Players/Keepers that are dropped during the season and added through waivers/free agency will be valued at an 11th Round Keeper **or the round they were originally drafted in, whichever is earlier.** This will be on a new three-year contract at the new keeper valuation.
      1. *Example 1:* In 2024, Tyler dropped Chase Brown (12th round pick in 2024) and added him back as a Waiver/Free Agent. His value is now reassigned as an 11th round keeper on a three-year contract.
      2. *Example 2:* In 2024, Tyler dropped Anthony Richardson (6th round pick in 2024) and David added him as a Waiver/Free Agent. His value is still as a 6th round keeper on a three-year contract.
         1. This is to prevent a 1st round talent from being dropped and using FAAB to add that player back as an 11th round keeper.
   3. You cannot drop your own player and use FAAB to reclaim them on waivers at a new keeper valuation on a longer contract.
      1. For example, Brendon has Bucky Irving as a 15th round keeper. In year three of Bucky Irving's contract, Brendon cannot drop Bucky Irving and use FAAB to reclaim him on a new three-year contract at an 11th round keeper value.
5. When a player is traded in fantasy, the recipient inherits the player's Keeper Contract as it stands with the previous owner. This includes the number of years left at the original draft round.
6. Examples:
   1. Dorian Thompson-Robinson is drafted in the 15th round in the 2024 offseason. After a breakout 2024 season, his value rises to a 2nd round pick the following year. He can still be kept at his original 15th round cost for the 2025 and 2026 offseasons. In the 2027 offseason, with an ADP of 14.1, his keeper cost increases to a 2nd round pick.
   2. In the example above, if DTR is traded during the 2025 season, the recipient inherits his Keeper Contract with one keeper year remaining at the 15th round value. The new team can keep him for the 2026 offseason at the 15th round cost, after which he will be revalued in the 2027 offseason.

### 1.8 Schedule

#### 1.8.1 Regular Season

The regular season will consist of 14 week-long matchups.

#### 1.8.2 Playoffs

1. The top 6 teams make playoffs
2. Playoffs are 3 weeks long, rounds last 1 week each
3. The top 2 teams receive byes
4. Seeding depending on standings and tie breaker is total Points For
5. If a playoff matchup is tied, the higher seeded team wins

#### 1.8.3 Week 18

Week 18 will not be used for fantasy purposes.

#### 1.8.4 Non Playoff Teams

Teams that do not make playoffs will enter a toilet bowl tournament: the losing team from each round gets pushed to the next round with the loser of the final round earning Last Place.

### 1.9 Prizes and Punishments

#### 1.9.1 Buy In

The yearly buy-in will be $50 per team.

#### 1.9.2 Prize Distribution

The total sum from team buy-ins will be distributed in the following manner:

- 1st place - 70%
- 2nd place - 20%
- 3rd place - 10%

#### 1.9.3 Last Place

The team to come in last place (loser of the toilet bowl championship) will be subject to punishment decided by league members before that year's draft.

1. 2024 Season punishment - Record yourself performing the NFL Combine.
2. 2025 Season punishment - *(TBD)*

#### 1.9.4 Enforcement

1. Failure to comply with buy-in or league punishment from previous year before the draft results in the loss of the team's 3rd round draft pick.
   1. Exceptions can be made for absence of feasibility of completion of punishment given decent effort to determine an alternative.
   2. Three total failures to comply with buy-ins or punishments in a 10 year span result in removal from the league.

### 1.10 Amendments

Amendments and changes to league rules and bylaws can be made via an official vote of 50%.

#### 1.10.1 Definition: Official Vote

A team makes an official proposal.

Another team, not the proposing team, "seconds" the proposal.

A vote is conducted and an approving vote of **at least** the determined percentage of the voting league teams vote in favor of the rule change. Each team gets 1 vote; teams can choose to abstain.

## Appendix A

The draft order is determined after the keeper deadline because of the scenario outlined below:

**Scenario 1: Draft order comes out before the keeper deadline.**

Jaymie has the following draft picks: 1.01, 6.10, 7.01, 12.10
Jaymie has the following planned keepers:

- 1st round: Ja'Marr Chase
- 6th round: Not Kept
- 7th round: Brock Bowers
- 12th round: Not Kept

Brendon has the following draft picks: 1.10, 6.01, 7.10, 12.01
Brendon has the following planned keepers:

- 1st round: Not Kept
- 6th round: James Conner
- 7th round: Not Kept
- 12th round: T.J. Hockenson

**Potential Trade:**

| Jaymie receives | Brendon receives |
| :---- | :---- |
| 1.10 (Chase keeper instead of at 1.01) | 1.01 (moves up from 1.10) |
| 6.01 (moves up from 6.10) | 6.10 (Conner keeper instead of at 6.01) |
| 7.10 (Bowers keeper instead of at 7.01) | 7.01 (moves up from 7.10) |
| 12.01 (moves up from 12.10) | 12.10 (Hockenson keeper instead of at 12.01) |

Both teams would be improving their draft positions for non-keeper rounds while moving their keepers to the end of their respective rounds. This benefits both of their teams at no additional cost while hurting the rest of the league, which is now drafting behind them in each of these rounds. If the draft order was revealed before the keeper deadline, it would be a scramble for everyone to trade their keeper picks for better picks in non-keeper rounds. **To prevent this from happening, draft order is determined after the keeper deadline. Also, keeper draft picks are manually assigned and frozen from trading immediately after the keeper deadline.**

## Implementation notes

Not part of the bylaws. This maps the rules above onto the code, and records
where the two do not line up, so a future change to either can be checked
against the other.

### Verified against real data

Sleeper's league settings match the bylaws on roster shape (17 + 2 IR, and IR
excludes suspended players per 1.4.1), $100 FAAB, $0 minimum bid, Wednesday
waivers with a 2-day clear, a 1-day trade review, and `playoff_week_start: 15`
giving a 14-week regular season.

Bylaws 1.7.2.4.2 is confirmed by the data: of 38 keepers in 2025, 36 match their
2024 draft round exactly, and both exceptions are drop-and-re-add — Chase Brown
R12 to R11 and Jakobi Meyers R16 to R11.

### Open discrepancies

1. **1.7.2.4.2 contradicts 1.7.2.4.3.** 4.2's Example 1 has Tyler dropping and
   re-adding his own Chase Brown for a fresh R11 contract; 4.3 says you cannot
   drop your own player to reclaim on a longer contract. The code implements
   4.2, because that is what actually happened in 2025. Parked by the league.
2. **Trade deadline.** Bylaws say Week 11; Sleeper's 2025 league had
   `trade_deadline: 12`, and 2024 had none at all. `config/rules/*` says 11.
3. **ADP beyond pick 170** converts to a round past 17, which cannot be a keeper
   cost in a 10-team, 17-round draft. First bites in 2027.
4. **1.9.3** — the 2025 last-place punishment is still blank.

### League history predates these bylaws

The bylaws describe a 10-team league. It was 12 teams from 2020 to 2023 on
ESPN, with a consolation LADDER rather than the anti-tournament in 1.8.4 —
there, winning moved you up a rung. Each season's own rules file carries its
team count, and imported brackets are marked so the two formats never merge.
